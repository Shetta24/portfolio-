T-SQL · Customer analytics

Customer Analytics & Segmentation Report

One SQL view that turns raw sales and customer tables into a ready-to-use customer report: who the customers are, how they buy and which segment they belong to.

  • Joins the sales fact table with the customer dimension using CTEs
  • Segments customers as VIP, Regular or New, plus age groups, with CASE logic
  • Calculates recency, average order value and average monthly spend
CASE
  WHEN lifespan >= 12 AND total_sales > 5000 THEN 'VIP'
  WHEN lifespan >= 12 AND total_sales <= 5000 THEN 'Regular'
  ELSE 'New'
END AS customer_segment

Sales & Customer Analytics

A multi-part SQL analysis of sales, product and customer data to uncover business insights: sales trends, customer behaviour, product performance and category contribution.

  • Monthly sales, customers and items sold, and how they change over time
  • Cumulative analysis with window functions, and yearly product performance against the average
  • Year-over-year change with LAG(), category share of total sales, plus product-cost and customer segmentation

  PARTITION BY product_name
